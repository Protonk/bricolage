# Disposable output

Contents of this directory may be removed at any time. Do not keep source
assets, durable records, or anything that must survive here. Preserve this
checked-in `AGENTS.md` when clearing output; everything else is disposable and
Git-ignored.

The experiment's comparison reports are written here when a command names a
destination under `output/`. Sampler worlds are written under
`Fixtures/output/` instead; see [its guidance](../Fixtures/output/AGENTS.md).
