# Helping assemble a new fixture

Help us assemble one new image fixture by supplying a clear brief and traceable
source material. A fixture is a collection of images with recorded identities
and a stated reason for inclusion. You can contribute without seeing our local
repository; the local agent will handle its structure and tools.

Start with what the collection should contain, its approximate size, and any
important exclusions or acceptable variation. Explain the basis for inclusion:
a visual criterion, membership in a published collection, or a documented
relationship among images. Preserve a published collection's definitions. For
relationship fixtures, describe the actual connection, such as transformations
of one source or separate views of one object, and the evidence supporting it.

Find a bounded starting population that the local agent can recover. Give exact
category or collection names and links, including relevant years, query settings,
whether subcategories are included, and any preselected rejects. Label counts
as observed or estimated and say when you checked. A source page or reproducible
query is enough to start. An existing export or list of file-page links is useful
when readily available; no special data format is required. If access or export
is blocked, describe the limitation and pass on the usable leads.

When supplying individual candidates, retain their source-page URLs and exact
titles or identifiers. Include credit and reuse information you encountered,
with links to its evidence. Say what you actually checked: search results, source
pages, thumbnails or downloaded files. Apply only the review criteria in the
brief, and give a short reason for any proposed rejection. Leave unseen images
unreviewed and unknown facts unknown. Note evident duplicates or related captures;
their treatment depends on the fixture's purpose.

Deliver a short prose brief with the source links, any available original exports,
your observations and unresolved questions. Distinguish suggestions from verified
findings, and describe the material supplied as leads, reviewed candidates or
acquired files as appropriate. Report a shortfall rather than widening the brief
to meet a count.

The local agent preserves your handoff, records the candidate population, applies
the agreed selection method, verifies individual source identities and rights
evidence, and acquires images using the current local workflow. It preserves
acquired bytes and creates the fixture records, previews and checks. Repository
paths, file naming, manifests, acquisition code and validation belong to that
local integration step.
