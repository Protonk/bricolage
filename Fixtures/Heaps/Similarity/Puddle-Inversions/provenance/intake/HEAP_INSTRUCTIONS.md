# PI01 intake contract

This contribution follows the `prompt_heap` sourcing and acquisition contract in
[Pale-Skies-Dark-Ground's instructions](../../../Pale-Skies-Dark-Ground/provenance/intake/HEAP_INSTRUCTIONS.md):
intent first, actual preview inspection, independent visual and transport axes,
preserved identities, evidence layout, provenance checks, conservative Wikimedia
access with no automatic retries, reported versus decoded dimensions, and a
handoff of data and bytes rather than a matching claim.

## PI01 specifics

- **Element label:** `puddle-inversions` (`PI01`); fixture ID `bricolage-pi01-v1`.
- **Brief:** the supplied [handoff README](../supplied/README.md), preserved unchanged.
  Its 45 file-page leads from `Category:Reflections in puddles` are the closed
  candidate population; subcategories were not traversed and no lead is added here.
- **Visual prompt, looseness and exclusions:** [prompt.json](prompt.json),
  taken from the brief. Loose selection is intended; an ordinary scene with a
  small puddle can stay, and the heap may lean toward some subjects by accident.
- **Review:** the local integrator inspects each acquired 320px preview and
  records accept, reject or defer with a short visual reason. The sourcing
  partner inspected no pixels. Rights for the 26 listing-only leads come from
  the Commons API reply at intake, not from the handoff.
- **Rights:** Commons license short names are recorded as reported. PI-20's
  institutional "no known copyright restrictions" statement is retained in that
  form and never restated as public domain.
- **Siblings:** the partner withheld same-site series and the cropped derivative
  of `Puddle Hopping.jpg` ([preselected-rejects.md](../supplied/preselected-rejects.md)).
  Remaining relationship questions are decided after preview inspection and
  recorded in `provenance/source-review.json`.
- **Size:** requested 35–50; 45 leads supplied; no single target number. A final
  set below 35 is reported as a shortfall, not widened.
- **Identity:** `BricolageFixtureBot/0.1 (https://github.com/Protonk/bricolage) Python-urllib`,
  `maxlag=5`, at least five seconds between requests, serial chunks of twelve
  after a one-image pilot at each stage.
